Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026.

What this site collects

Spark & Duct does not ask for your name, email address or any account. The calculators and quizzes run entirely in your browser, and the numbers and answers you enter are not sent to the site or stored by it.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts at present.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser requests a page, including your IP address, and may keep it in server logs for security purposes. GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Advertising

The site may show advertising in the future to cover its costs. Advertising providers commonly use cookies to choose and measure ads. If advertising is added, this page will be updated first to name the provider, explain what it collects and link to the controls it offers.

Links to other sites

Pages may link to manufacturers, licensing boards and other outside sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
