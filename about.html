About Spark & Duct

Free tools and plain answers for people who work on low voltage, electrical and HVAC systems.

What this site is

Spark & Duct is a set of calculators, guides, fault code lookups and reference charts for technicians and installers. It is built to be used on a phone, at a panel or on a roof, without an account and without waiting on a slow page.

Who it is for

  • Fire alarm and low voltage technicians sizing batteries and checking notification circuits.
  • Electricians checking voltage drop and conduit fill.
  • HVAC technicians checking airflow and reading furnace fault codes.
  • Apprentices and students studying for certification exams.

How the tools are made

Every calculator shows the formula it uses, so you can check the result by hand. Calculations run in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

The site grew out of field work in low voltage systems, residential HVAC and plant controls. It is written with the help of AI tools, and results are checked against hand calculations before a tool is published.

Limits

This site is for estimating and study. It does not replace the adopted code in your area, the manufacturer's instructions, a licensed professional's judgment or the authority having jurisdiction. Table values are reference figures; confirm them against the current edition of the code before relying on them.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please report it through the contact page. Corrections are welcome and get fixed quickly.

For estimating and study only. Check everything against the current code, the manufacturer's data and the authority having jurisdiction before you build or repair.
