How it is worked out

V = I × R and P = V × I. Every other form comes from rearranging those two:

  • I = V ÷ R, R = V ÷ I
  • P = I² × R, P = V² ÷ R

These hold for DC circuits and for AC circuits with a purely resistive load, such as heating elements. Motors and other inductive loads also have a power factor, so their real power is lower than volts times amps.

A quick field use: a 24 V relay coil that measures 120 Ω should draw 0.2 A and dissipate 4.8 W.
