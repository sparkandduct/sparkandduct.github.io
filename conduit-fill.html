How it is worked out

Allowed fill follows NEC Chapter 9 Table 1: 53 % for one conductor, 31 % for two and 40 % for three or more. Conduit areas come from Table 4 and THHN areas from Table 5.

A multiconductor cable counts as one conductor. Enter its outside diameter from the cable data sheet; for a flat or oval cable use the larger dimension.

The 60 % allowance for nipples of 24 inches or less is not applied here.
