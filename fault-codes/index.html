Fault codes

What the blinking light means, and what to check first.

  • Carrier furnace fault codesTwo-digit LED status codes for Carrier, Bryant and Payne gas furnaces.
  • Goodman furnace fault codesLED flash counts for Goodman and Amana gas furnaces.

Code lists differ between control boards. The chart on the inside of the unit's access panel is the one that applies to that unit.

For estimating and study only. Check everything against the current code, the manufacturer's data and the authority having jurisdiction before you build or repair.
