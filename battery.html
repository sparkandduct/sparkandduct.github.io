How it is worked out

Ah = (standby amps × standby hours + alarm amps × alarm hours) × safety factor

NFPA 72 calls for 24 hours of standby followed by 5 minutes of alarm for most systems, and 15 minutes of alarm for voice evacuation. The 1.2 factor is the 20 % margin the standard requires. Some panels and jurisdictions ask for more, so check the panel manual and the local authority.

A 24 V panel uses two 12 V batteries in series. The amp-hour rating of the pair is the rating of one battery.
