Found a mistake or want a tool added?

The quickest way to reach the site is to open an issue on its public project page:

github.com/sparkandduct/sparkandduct.github.io/issues

What helps

  • For a wrong result: the page, the numbers you entered, what you got and what you expected.
  • For a wrong code or table value: the value and the source that shows the right one.
  • For a new tool: what you need to work out and how you do it by hand today.
