The factors used

  • 1 ton of cooling = 12,000 BTU/h
  • 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/h
  • 1 psi = 27.68 inches of water column = 6.895 kPa
  • 1 inch of water column = 249.1 Pa
  • 1 CFM = 0.472 L/s = 1.699 m³/h
  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9

Gas pressures on furnace nameplates are given in inches of water column: typically about 3.5 for natural gas and 10 for propane at the manifold. Always use the figure on the nameplate.
