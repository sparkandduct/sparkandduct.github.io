Goodman furnace fault codes

LED flash counts used on Goodman and Amana gas furnaces with a single diagnostic light.

Code lists differ between control boards, and newer Goodman furnaces with a two-character display use different codes entirely. The chart inside the furnace's access panel is the one that applies to that furnace.

How to read the code

Look at the LED on the control board through the sight glass in the lower door. Count the flashes in a group; the group repeats after a pause. Read it before switching power off, since that can clear the code.

The three you will see most

1 flash: ignition lockout

Watch a start-up. If the igniter glows, the burners light and then go out within a few seconds, the flame sensor is not proving flame; cleaning it usually cures that. If the igniter never glows, check the igniter. If it glows but nothing lights, check the gas supply and valve.

3 flashes: pressure switch open

The control is not seeing proof that the vent is drawing. On condensing furnaces, water trapped in the pressure tubing or a blocked drain trap is the common cause. Check the vent termination outside for ice, snow or nests.

4 flashes: high limit open

Start with the filter. If it is clean, confirm the blower runs and measure the temperature rise against the nameplate range.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility. A furnace that keeps tripping a safety switch has a fault that needs finding, not resetting.
