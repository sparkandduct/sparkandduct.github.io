How it is worked out

Single-phase: amps = VA ÷ volts

Three-phase: amps = VA ÷ (1.732 × line-to-line volts)

A 40 VA, 24 V control transformer can supply 40 ÷ 24 = 1.67 A. Add up the VA of every relay, contactor coil, thermostat and accessory on it; if the total is over the rating, the transformer runs hot and the voltage sags.

A 75 kVA three-phase transformer at 480 V to 208 V carries 90.2 A on the primary and 208.2 A on the secondary at full load.

These are full-load currents only. Overcurrent protection and conductor sizes for transformers are set by the electrical code, which allows percentages above full-load current that depend on the transformer and where the protection is placed.
