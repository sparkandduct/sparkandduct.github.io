EPA 608 practice exams

Four exams of 25 questions each: Core, Type I, Type II and Type III. Print the exams, take them closed-book, then score yourself with the answer key at the end. All questions are original.

How to use these

  • The real tests have at least 25 Core questions and at least 25 questions per technical group (40 CFR 82.161(c)). Each exam here matches that length.
  • Take Core first. Add the technical exam for the equipment you work on. A Universal certificate means passing all four.
  • Pass marks are set by the testing organization you use, so check with them. These pages do not give one.
  • Print with the answer key left out by choosing pages 1 to 8 in the print dialog (the exams), then print the rest separately.
  • Evacuation levels, recovery percentages and leak rates here follow the federal rule (40 CFR part 82, subpart F). Leak repair questions cover section 82.157, which applies to equipment with 50 pounds or more of ozone-depleting refrigerant. Rules that apply to HFC equipment under 40 CFR part 84, and any state rules, are not covered.

Answer keys

Score yourself, then read the explanation for every miss. Cover this page while you test.

Core answers

  1. B. Chlorine. UV light breaks chlorine atoms off CFC and HCFC molecules, and the chlorine then depletes ozone.
  2. D. CFC, then HCFC, then HFC. CFCs have the highest ODP, HCFCs are lower (0.01 to 0.1), and HFCs have an ODP of zero.
