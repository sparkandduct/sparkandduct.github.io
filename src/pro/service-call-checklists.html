HVAC service call checklists

Two sheets: a cooling call and a heating call. Print both sides, one sheet per page. Targets come from the unit's data plate and the manufacturer's instructions, so the Target column is blank.

Cooling service call

Check each item as you finish it. Write readings in the Value column.

Date: ____________   Address: ______________________________   Unit make and model: ______________________

  • Customer complaint, in their words, and when it started
  • Power off, locked out at the disconnect, and verified with a meter before panels come off
  • Thermostat: mode, setpoint, fan setting, batteries, display
  • Air filter: clean, correct size, correct direction
  • All supply registers and return grilles open and unblocked
  • Indoor coil: visible dirt or frost on the coil or the suction line
  • Blower wheel and blower motor: clean, spins freely
  • Condensate drain and float switch: clear, no standing water in the pan
  • Outdoor coil: clean, fins straight, clearance on all sides
  • Condenser fan: spins freely, correct direction, blades secure
  • Contactor contacts: no pitting or burning
  • Run capacitor: measured uF against the label, case not bulged
  • Wiring and connections: tight, no burned or discolored terminals
  • Refrigerant lines: insulation intact, no oil stains at joints or fittings
  • Power restored; unit started; run at least 10 minutes before taking readings
  • Readings recorded below and compared with the data plate and manufacturer's charging instructions
  • Panels back on, filter in, thermostat set back to the customer's setting
  • Findings explained to the customer, with what was done and what is recommended

Heating service call

Check each item as you finish it. Write readings in the Value column.

Date: ____________   Address: ______________________________   Unit make and model: ______________________

  • Customer complaint, in their words, and when it started
  • Thermostat: mode, setpoint, fan setting, batteries, display
  • Power off, locked out at the disconnect, and verified with a meter before panels come off
  • Air filter: clean, correct size, correct direction
  • All supply registers and return grilles open and unblocked
  • Blower wheel and blower motor: clean, spins freely
  • Furnace cabinet and door: door switch pressed when the panel is on
  • Flue pipe: connected, sloped as the instructions require, no rust streaks or gaps
  • Combustion air and vent termination: not blocked by snow, leaves or nests
  • Condensate drain and trap (condensing furnace): clear and primed
  • Flame sensor: clean, tight, no white coating
  • Burners: flame steady and blue, all burners light, no rollout marks
  • Igniter: no cracks, correct glow
  • Heat exchanger: visual check with a light, no cracks or holes seen
  • Gas piping and connections: leak check done with a gas detector or approved leak solution
  • Fault code from the board LED or display written down before power is cycled
  • Carbon monoxide check at the supply registers and at the flue, per the manufacturer's instructions
  • Power restored; run a full heating cycle; record readings below
  • Panels back on, filter in, thermostat set back to the customer's setting
  • Findings explained to the customer, with what was done and what is recommended
